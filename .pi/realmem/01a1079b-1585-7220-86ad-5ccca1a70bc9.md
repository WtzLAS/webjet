In crates/webjetd/src/gossip.rs `on_bucket_index`, removals were computed only for buckets present in the peer's BucketIndex reply (`theirs.keys()`). When a leaf deletion left its bucket empty on the sender's side, that bucket had no entries, so the receiver never removed its stale leaf, `install()` failed with a root mismatch and the node stayed one tree version behind forever (seen when clearing a NodeConfig leaf). Fix: `Walk.buckets` records the differing buckets sent in GetBucketIndex, and removals cover all of them. Also since 0.4.0, `leaf::decode` returns `LeafValue::Unknown` for unknown leaf kinds (kept and hashed, ignored) so future leaf kinds don't break sync of older nodes; 0.3.x nodes still choke on kind 9 (NODE_CONFIG).
