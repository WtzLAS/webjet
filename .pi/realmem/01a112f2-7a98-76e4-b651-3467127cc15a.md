The problem: a restarted node that redialed over UDP was refused as a duplicate for up to HOP_DEAD (15 s), because its peer still held the dead KCP hop. RPCs in that window timed out; this was the docker/webjetup-test.sh step 6c "opted out: wrong error" failure.

Fix in `crates/webjetd/src/node.rs`: `keep_new_hop(me, peer, outbound, existing_outbound, existing_age) -> Dup {Replace, PeerLost, Refuse}`.
- A dial race is one inbound plus one outbound hop within DIAL_RACE = 2 s. In a race the lower node id's outbound wins.
- Any other inbound duplicate is `PeerLost`. The old hop is retired (no flap recorded), then `hop_down`, then `circuit::close_with_neighbor`.

To reproduce, use /tmp/restart-rpc.sh (not committed): local-net with one node, restart node-02 and time `network node upgrade-status`. It was 15–30 s before the fix and is 3 s after. Fixed in v0.9.0.
