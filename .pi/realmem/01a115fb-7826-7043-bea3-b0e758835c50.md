Since commit 72b2b34 (2026-10), `crates/webjet-app/src/graph.rs` is a directory.
- `graph/mod.rs`: Vertex, Edge, Graph (build/keep/focus), Viewport, and the refresh tests (`graph::tests`).
- `graph/layout.rs`: DEFAULT_AREA, `vertex_box`, `Item`, `layout`, `place`, Part, Cost, anneal, improve, separate and pack, plus their tests (`graph::layout::tests`, including the `svg` helper for WEBJET_LAYOUT_SVG).
- mod.rs re-exports `DEFAULT_AREA` and `vertex_box`. `Item::extent`, `P` and `place` are `pub(super)`.
- Test filter `graph::` covers both test modules.
