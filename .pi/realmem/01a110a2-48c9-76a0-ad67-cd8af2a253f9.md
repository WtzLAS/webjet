`crates/webjetd/src/circuit.rs` `on_open`: `admit(n, &src, to_me)` applies `circuit_setups_per_sec` and `circuits_per_source` to circuits that END at this node as well (Local entries with `!is_source && peer == src`); `circuits_global` only counts transit. Before, `accept_as_destination` had no limits at all. Relays: when the next hop has no session, `extend()` (dial + transit entries + forward CircuitOpen) runs in `tokio::spawn`, because `on_open` is awaited inside the previous hop's reader loop and `dial_node` can take 10 s per address/transport (stalled all circuits and keepalives on that link). Sources keep ~2 circuits per peer (paths.rs MAX_CIRCUITS) plus trials every 30 s, well under the limits.
