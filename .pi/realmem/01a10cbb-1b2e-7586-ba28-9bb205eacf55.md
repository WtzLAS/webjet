FEC in Webjet is not a switch: it is always active on KCP hops and adaptive (`crates/webjet-net/src/fec.rs`, ratio 0–30% from the peer's K_STATS loss feedback). Before the fix (after 0.5.1), the metrics `fec_ratio_pct`, `fec_recovered` and `kcp_retrans` always showed 0:
- the receiver sent K_STATS with received == expected (only counted shards that arrived), so the measured loss was 0, the sender's ratio stayed 0, no parity went out, nothing could be recovered;
- `KcpStats::retrans` was never written (the `kcp` 0.6 crate keeps its `xmit` counter private and counts only timeouts, not fast resends).
Fix: `fec::Decoder` measures line loss before recovery from group gaps (`take_loss()`; settle lag 4 groups; a group's size comes from its parity header `k`, otherwise from its highest data index; whole-group losses use the average k; gaps > 256 groups are treated as an outage and skipped). `kcp::count_retransmits` parses KCP output packets (24-byte LE header, cmd 81 = PUSH, sn at offset 12, len at 20) and counts a PUSH whose sn is below the next new sn. Test `kcp::tests::lossy_link_turns_on_fec` runs two endpoints through a UDP proxy that drops every 10th datagram.
