The relay capacity auto-estimate (`capacity_mbps` in crates/webjetd/src/metrics.rs = max(peak sustained throughput, max KCP `bw_estimate_kbps`, 100)) showed 4000+ Mbit/s on a 200 Mbit/s relay. Cause, in crates/webjet-net/src/kcp.rs `run_session`: BBR delivery-rate samples were taken between two loop turns (often ~1 ms apart) whenever `wait_snd` shrank, and counted every acked segment as a full KCP_MTU. Acknowledgements arrive in bursts, so a 20 Mbit/s path read as ~500 Mbit/s. Fix: the `Delivery` struct gathers acked bytes (real payload lengths of new PUSH segments, recorded by `scan_output`, formerly `count_retransmits`) into one sample covering at least `max(bbr.min_rtt, MIN_SAMPLE_INTERVAL = 25 ms)`. A burst delivered right before the send buffer empties counts as application-limited. Tests: `kcp::tests::bandwidth_estimate_follows_the_path_rate` (a 20 Mbit/s shaping UDP proxy; the peak estimate must stay below 2×) and `delivery_samples_span_the_minimum_interval`.
