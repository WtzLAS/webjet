Since commit 82be796 (2026-10 code-review round C), the shared helpers have a single definition each:
- `webjet_core::crypto::{b64, unb64}`: standard base64 with padding.
- `webjet_core::types::now_ms`.

Crates that re-export instead of keeping their own copy:
- `webjetd::state` does `pub use webjet_core::crypto::{b64, unb64}`.
- `webjet_tools` does `pub use webjet_core::crypto::b64`.
- `webjet_release` does `pub use webjet_core::types::now_ms` and imports b64/unb64. It no longer depends on base64 directly.
- `webjet-core` sntp.rs and webjet-app ui/mod.rs import `now_ms`.

Don't add new local copies.

Also from round C: `gossip::handle` and `dataplane::start` are now sync (no `.await`).
