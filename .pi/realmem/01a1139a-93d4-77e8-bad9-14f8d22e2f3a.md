`crates/webjet-core/src/statemachine.rs`: `Machine::check_new_entry(payload)` runs only in `prepare_entry` and `check` (after `execute`), never in `apply`. Use it for tightened validation that must not make replicas diverge on entries already in the log (no StateVersion step needed). Current checks (2026-10 Phase 2):
- published prefixes reserved by pending enrollments (CreateEnrollment / UpdateNode with set_routes refused if overlapping a pending enrollment's grant, identical grant allowed); RedeemEnrollmentEntry re-runs `check_routes` against current nodes;
- `refuse_shadowing`: DNS records in SetNetworkConfig / SetNodeConfig may not equal `<node>.<suffix>` case-insensitively and without trailing dot.
Putting such checks into `execute` instead would reject old committed entries on replay → Divergence halt.
