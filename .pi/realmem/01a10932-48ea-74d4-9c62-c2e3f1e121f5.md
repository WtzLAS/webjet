Nodes graph (crates/webjet-app/src/ui/nodes.rs + graph.rs): `graph::Viewport { pan, zoom }` in fractions of the drawing area (to_view/to_graph/zoom_at around pointer, zoom 0.2–8). WebjetApp holds `viewport`, `pinned: BTreeMap<node id,(x,y)>` (re-applied by `Graph::pin` after every refresh), `drag: Option<Drag>` (Pan or Node(id), 3 px click slop: press+release without moving = select), `graph_bounds: Rc<Cell<[f32;4]>>` written by the edges canvas prepaint. Vertex on_mouse_down starts a node drag + `cx.stop_propagation()`; area on_mouse_down starts a pan; while dragging the canvas paint registers window-wide `window.on_mouse_event` MouseMove/MouseUp listeners (drag continues outside the area). Scroll: pixel deltas pan, Lines or ⌘/Ctrl zoom. Buttons −/+/Fit/Re-layout.

GPUI 0.2.2 test gotchas (ui::tests::every_tab_renders): simulated events hit the window's own rendered frame, not what `cx.draw(...)` painted — call `cx.simulate_resize(size(1280,820))` first, and between events redraw with `view.update(cx, |_, cx| cx.notify()); cx.run_until_parked();` rather than `cx.draw`. Overlapping vertex labels mean the grabbed node may not be the one you aimed at: read the id from `app.drag`. Calling `app.loaded(fixture())` drops the test admin account → signed out and the window renders the sign-in screen; reuse the snapshot that has the admin added.
