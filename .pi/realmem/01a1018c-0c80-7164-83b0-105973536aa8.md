Metrics are pull-only OpenMetrics (no remote write): `[metrics] listen` serves GET /metrics (crates/webjetd/src/exposition.rs); on a controller it serves every node's latest report — nodes upload signed reports to a controller, controllers forward reports from their origin node to the other controllers, so any controller is a full scrape target. Relay load (§11.8.1) = max of util/cpu/circuits/queue pct, 30 s averages from `load.rs` LoadSampler (real process CPU via getrusage/GetProcessTimes); capacity auto-estimated unless configured (≥100 Mbit/s); LSA republished on a 10% load step at most every 10 s. Link cost: `LinkStats` (srtt/rttvar/min RTT/window loss) plus persistent `LinkHistory` per (peer, transport) in `links.json` (15-min EWMAs, flap count with 1 h half-life, stability factor ≤2×, new sessions warm up from history).
