In crates/webjet-app/src/ui/mod.rs, `scroll(id)` sets `flex_1` (grow 1, shrink 1, basis 0). Adding `.w(px(N)).flex_shrink_0()` to it inside a row does NOT fix its width: the zero basis + grow makes it share leftover space with neighbours (the Nodes tab's 380px node-side column rendered ~950px wide, squeezing the graph/circuit details). Use `side_scroll(id, width)` (flex_none + flex_basis + w) for fixed-width side columns (node-side 420, circuit-list 440 shrinkable to 300, sessions-side 340, merkle-detail 380).
Rows of legend-like items need `.flex_wrap()` plus `flex_none()/whitespace_nowrap()` children, otherwise children overflow past the panel while the container itself still measures inside.
Layout regression test: `ui::tests::nodes_tab_fits` resizes the window (1435×852, 1280×820, 1100×760) and checks element bounds via `.debug_selector(|| "name".into())` + `cx.debug_bounds("name")` (only works with gpui test-support; noop in release). Measure the last child, not the container, to catch overflow.
