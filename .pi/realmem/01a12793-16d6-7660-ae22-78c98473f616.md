In docker/*.sh tests, `on_host "webjetup install" | grep -q X` is racy: grep -q exits at the first match, the `docker exec` client dies on EPIPE, but webjetup keeps running inside the container and may rewrite files/restart services while the next test step runs. Capture to a file first (`on_host … >/tmp/$P-x.txt 2>&1; grep -q X /tmp/$P-x.txt`). Also: right after `systemctl restart`, /proc/<MainPID>/status may not yet show the exec'd process's CapAmb — poll (`expect_caps` in docker/webjetup-test.sh). docker/webjetup-test.sh runs on Linux hosts too (e.g. root@10.4.0.9 in /root/webjet-lane, rsync the tree there); bsdtar-only flags are gated on Darwin.
