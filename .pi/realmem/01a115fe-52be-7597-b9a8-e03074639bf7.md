Since commit de5f7af (2026-10, Phase 6), `crates/webjetd/src/controller/` has these files beside raft/audit/recovery/signer:
- `requests.rs`: `handle_rpc`, `on_raft`, `log`, `export_snapshot`, `submit_result`.
- `enroll.rs`: `pending_token_hashes`, `redeem`, `redeem_self`, `serve_enrollment`, `enroll_response`, `cert_request`.
- `leader.rs`: `leader_duties`, `auto_membership*`, `membership_plan`, `membership_settings_json`.
- `reports.rs`: `StoredReport` (re-exported from mod.rs), `ingest_metrics`, `openmetrics`.
- `mod.rs` keeps the Controller struct, start/run, `apply_committed`, `maybe_sign_head`, propose/submit, status, `classify_entry` + `conf_change`, `root_payload`, and the tests. The child modules use `use super::*;`, and their methods are `pub(super)` unless they were pub.

Also in Phase 6 (commit 809a83d): `crates/webjetd/src/admin.rs::signed_request(view, raw, ty, what, account_fn)` is the one check for admin-signed LogRequest, SnapshotRequest and UpgradeRequest. A wrong type gives the error "expected a signed X, got Y". `control/mod.rs::submitted()` decodes a SubmitResult/RpcAck reply.
