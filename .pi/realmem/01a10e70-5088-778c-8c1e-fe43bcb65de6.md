In webjet-app (GPUI 0.2.2), a plain text child of a `label_value` value div could paint lines over the row below in the real app (screenshot QQ20261006-064820: circuit details Path/Kind/Round trip overlapping). The cause: taffy measures text several times and caches the results, so the paragraph is painted wrapped at a different width than the one its height was measured for. The headless test (scale 2.0) could not reproduce it. Fix in `crates/webjet-app/src/ui/mod.rs`: `label_value` now renders `wrapped(text)`. `pieces()` splits each line into words plus their trailing space, and `cut_word` breaks long words after `-` `.` `:` `/` `,` or every PIECE=12 chars. Each piece is a `flex_none().whitespace_nowrap()` div inside a `flex().flex_wrap()` row, so pieces never wrap themselves and the measured size always matches what is painted. The value div is `flex_1().min_w(0)` and has `debug_selector("value-<Label>")`. Tests: `values_wrap_between_words` and `circuit_details_do_not_overlap` (checks that the rows' bounds don't overlap at several window widths).
